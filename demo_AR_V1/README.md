# Second Entry demo (AR V1, "Lineup" design)

A clickable demo website for Second Entry, with sample data only. No real payments or accounts.

**To view it:** download `index.html` and open it in a browser, or open it on your phone. It works at both phone and desktop sizes.

## What's in it

- **Homepage:** hero with event search, upcoming events listed by date, how it works, the money-held guarantee, a section for venues and an FAQ.
- **Event pages:** listings with a "Never above face value" badge, seller verification and the all-in price including the protection fee.
- **Checkout:** PromptPay QR or card (simulated). Money is held until the buyer confirms they got in, with an auto-confirm countdown and a "Report a problem" option.
- **Sell a ticket:** three steps, with the price capped at face value and a status timeline.
- **Team review desk** (footer link): approve or ban listings, release payouts or refund buyers.
- **Pilot & data** (footer link): counters, an activity log, a feedback log by channel, the outreach draft and a JSON copy button.
- **Thai and English toggle.** Progress is saved in the browser, and "Reset demo" is in the footer.

Fees: 30 baht for tickets up to 1,000 baht, otherwise 3% capped at 100 baht.
